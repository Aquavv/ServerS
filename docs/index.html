ServerS

A simple tool to block high ping servers in Overwatch 2.

Play on your own terms

If you're tired of being thrown into distant lobbies with bad latency, ServerS lets you fix that. It uses Windows Firewall to block the IPs of servers you don't want (like those annoying AWS or GCP nodes), forcing the game to put you in a local server.

  • No Bans: Safe to use. It doesn't touch game files or memory.
  • Easy to use: Check the servers you hate, and hit apply.
  • Live Ping: See your actual latency before you even open the game.
